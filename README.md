# housing-risks-by-income
Title: Housing Quality Risks by Income in the United States from 2011 - 2023 (biennial).

Research Question: How do housing quality–related safety risks, a key social determinant of health, vary by household income in the United States from 2011 to 2023 (biennial)?

Dataset
Comes from American Housing Survey (AHS) https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/ahs/data/interactive/ahstablecreator.html
The housing conditions used are: 
Mold
Water leakage in roofs and basements
External damages
Electrical problems
Exposed wiring

Methods

Cleans and reformats 7 AHS datasets from 2011-2023
Converts estimate values to numeric formats
Applies log transformations for skewed data 'estimate.'

Key Findings
. Housing quality risks vary across different household income groups.
. Issues like water leakage and structural problems need to be evaluated as they're issues related to socioeconomic inequality.
